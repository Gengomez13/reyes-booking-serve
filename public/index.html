Services We Provide

Adult Counseling

Our adult counseling services address various emotional and mental health challenges. We specialize in therapy for anxiety, depression, trauma, stress management, life transitions, and relationship issues. Using evidence-based approaches, we work with adults in the workplace to personal relationships, helping you build resilience and achieve emotional well-being.

Child Counseling

Our child counseling services provide comprehensive support for children ages 3-12 who face emotional, behavioral, or adjustment challenges. We help children manage anxiety, depression, behavioral issues, adjustment to changes, and social difficulties. Our child psychologists use play therapy and other age-appropriate techniques to help children understand and successfully navigate their emotions and experiences.

Contact Us

Teen Counseling

Our teen counseling services are specifically designed for adolescents ages 13-18. We address depression, anxiety, self-esteem issues, peer pressure, identity development, family conflicts, academic stress, substance use issues, and school-related challenges. Our specialized therapists help teens develop healthy coping skills and strategies for managing the unique demands and pressures of adolescence.

Contact Us

EMDR Therapy

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is an evidence-based therapeutic approach for trauma and PTSD. Through bilateral stimulation and eye movements, EMDR helps process traumatic memories, reduce emotional distress, and facilitate healing. This specialized therapy has been proven highly effective for treating trauma, anxiety disorders, depression, and other conditions related to distressing life experiences.

Contact Us

Couples Counseling

Our couples counseling services support relationships by addressing communication challenges, conflict resolution, intimacy issues, infidelity, life transitions, and relationship maintenance. We help couples strengthen their connection, improve understanding, develop healthy communication patterns, and create a more fulfilling relationship through evidence-based therapeutic interventions.
